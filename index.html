Neurodevelopmental Disorders in Children — Kenya Referral Tool

Young children with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (NDDs) who live in rural and underserved areas are often undiagnosed due to limited parental and teacher awareness, and lack of affordable psychiatric care. This tool helps parents aged 3–16 screen for possible conditions and find local support.

JAMA Network Open — Burden of NDDs in Kenyan Children

522 of 11,223 children aged 6–9 were diagnosed with a Neurodevelopmental Disorder, yet they had not received clinical help or support.

The Tool

Enter your child's symptoms, age, gender, and county to receive a probable condition (not a medical diagnosis) and a list of nearby hospitals, clinics, and programs.
