Project title: Neurodevelopmental Disorders in children aged 4-9 in Rural Areas

Young children aged 4-9 with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (NDDs') who live in Rural Areas who are undiagnosed due to lack of parental/teacher knowledge on the condition and affordable psychiatric care.

Jama Network: Open-Burden of Neurodevelopmental Disorders in Kenyan Children

522 of 11,223 of children aged 6-9 were diagnosed with a Neurodevelopmental Disorder, yet they have not received clinical help or support.

The tool

Type in your child's symptoms, age, gender and county in kenya to get a diagnosis. What symptoms does your child display? How old is your child currently? What gender is your child? What county does the child reside in?
