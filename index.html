Project title: Neurodevelopmental Disorders in children aged 4-9 across Kenya

Young children aged 4-9 with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (NDDs') across Kenya who are undiagnosed due to lack of parental/teacher knowledge on the condition and affordable psychiatric care.

Jama Network: Open-Burden of Neurodevelopmental Disorders in Kenyan Children

522 of 11,223 of children aged 6-9 were diagnosed with a Neurodevelopmental Disorder, yet they have not received clinical help or support.
